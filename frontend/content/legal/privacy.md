## 1. Introduction

Morena's Gown and Barong Rental and Sales Management System ("Morena's," "we," "our," or "us") values the privacy and security of its customers and website users.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, disclose, and protect personal information obtained through our website and related services.

We are committed to handling personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and other applicable Philippine data protection regulations.

By using our website or creating an account, you acknowledge that you have been informed of the data processing practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect the following categories of information:

### 2.1. Account Information

When registering for an account, we may collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Contact number
- Username or account identifier
- Password and authentication information

Passwords are stored using secure password-hashing mechanisms rather than as readable plain text.

### 2.2. Transaction Information

When renting or purchasing products, we may collect:

- Order and reservation details
- Selected products and quantities
- Rental dates and return dates
- Transaction amounts
- Payment status and payment references
- Order history
- Delivery or pickup information, where applicable

### 2.3. Additional Customer Information

When necessary for providing our services, we may collect:

- Billing and delivery addresses
- Customer measurements or sizing preferences, if provided
- Rental-related instructions
- Customer inquiries and support communications

### 2.4. Reviews and Feedback

When customers submit reviews, ratings, or feedback, we may collect the submitted content and information associated with the customer's account.

### 2.5. Technical Information

When users access the website, certain technical information may be collected, including:

- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Operating system
- Website activity and interaction logs
- Session and authentication information

Such information may be used for website functionality, security monitoring, troubleshooting, and performance improvement.

## 3. How We Collect Personal Information

We may collect personal information through the following methods:

3.1. When users register for an account or update their account information.

3.2. When customers browse, rent, or purchase products through the website.

3.3. When users submit reviews, feedback, inquiries, or support requests.

3.4. When customers complete transactions through integrated payment services.

3.5. Through website technologies, such as cookies, sessions, and system logs, where applicable.

## 4. Purpose of Collecting and Processing Information

We process personal information only for specified and legitimate purposes, which may include:

- Creating and managing customer accounts.
- Verifying user identity and account information.
- Processing rental reservations and purchase orders.
- Managing product availability and inventory.
- Processing and verifying payments.
- Monitoring rental periods, return schedules, and applicable late fees.
- Communicating order confirmations, updates, and transaction notifications.
- Providing customer support and responding to inquiries.
- Managing customer reviews and feedback.
- Maintaining website security and preventing fraudulent activities.
- Improving website functionality and user experience.
- Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Personal information shall not be processed for purposes incompatible with the purposes disclosed to customers, unless otherwise permitted by applicable law.

## 5. Payment Information and Third-Party Payment Providers

5.1. Morena's may use third-party payment service providers, including PayMongo and its supported payment channels, to facilitate online transactions.

5.2. Payment-related information may include transaction references, payment status, payment method, and other information necessary for transaction verification.

5.3. Sensitive payment credentials, such as complete card details, are handled by the applicable payment provider where its hosted or integrated payment services are used.

5.4. Morena's does not intend to store complete payment card credentials or banking passwords in its own application database.

5.5. Payment providers may independently process personal information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 6. AR Virtual Try-On and Camera Access

6.1. Morena's may provide an Augmented Reality (AR) Virtual Try-On feature that uses a device camera to display virtual garment representations.

6.2. Camera access may be requested when a customer chooses to use the AR Virtual Try-On feature.

6.3. Camera access is intended to support the AR experience and is not automatically required for ordinary account registration, browsing, or purchasing.

6.4. Where camera images or video frames are processed temporarily for virtual visualization, they shall be handled according to the actual technical implementation of the AR feature.

6.5. Any collection, storage, transmission, or further processing of photographs, recordings, or other camera-derived information shall be disclosed to users before such processing takes place.

6.6. Users may deny camera permission, although doing so may prevent them from accessing the AR Virtual Try-On feature.

## 10. Rights of Data Subjects

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, customers have rights concerning their personal information, subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations.

These rights include:

10.1. **Right to Be Informed** – The right to know whether personal information is being collected, processed, or stored.

10.2. **Right to Access** – The right to request reasonable access to personal information being processed.

10.3. **Right to Object** – The right to object to certain forms of processing, subject to lawful exceptions.

10.4. **Right to Rectification** – The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

10.5. **Right to Erasure or Blocking** – The right to request the removal, blocking, or destruction of personal information when legally justified.

10.6. **Right to Data Portability** – The right to obtain a copy of personal information in an accessible format when applicable.

10.7. **Right to Damages** – The right to seek compensation for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal information, as provided by law.

10.8. **Right to Lodge a Complaint** – The right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission when personal information has been misused or privacy rights have been violated.
